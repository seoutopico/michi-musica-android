Use case: ui-mockup.
Create a brand-new, exceptionally polished high-fidelity Android music app opening screen as a single flat front-facing screen image. Portrait approximately 1:2 aspect ratio, edge-to-edge UI, no device frame, no background outside the screen, no design annotations. Art direction: sophisticated Midnight music-library interface. Inky blue-black and deep plum surfaces, soft luminous dusty-rose accents, beautifully nuanced tonal depth. Crisp high-contrast warm-white typography. Contemporary Apple-level fit and finish, with native Android usability. Confident professional typography, immaculate 24dp-equivalent consistent lateral safe margins, generous but purposeful spacing, clear visual hierarchy. Depth comes from gently layered surfaces, subtle translucency in persistent controls, and rich album artwork, not heavy drop shadows or a repetitive stack of cards. Elegant photographic and abstract album covers make the music the visual focus. Covers are fictional sample content.

Product function: the user already has local songs, opens the app and can immediately choose one. A previous song is paused and can resume. Show songs directly on this screen. No welcome screen, no extra entry button, no huge decorative hero, no subscription or social features, no recommendation claims, no metadata editor.
